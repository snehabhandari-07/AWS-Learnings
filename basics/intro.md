# AWS - Amazon Web Services

## What is AWS?

AWS is a company famous for providing **cloud services**.

## What is Cloud?

- It’s **not** the cloud in the sky, but a space(virtual- it exists but not physically infront of u/ digital space) used by millions and billions of people worldwide.

## Why Cloud?

At first, when I heard about the cloud, I wasn’t very amazed by the concept. But as a computer engineering student, I started understanding its importance.

I love clicking photos. My phone was full of photos and videos of my friends and family. Eventually, my phone storage ran out, and I had to delete some memories. Then, I discovered Google Photos — where you can upload your photos online. I was overjoyed!

This is what cloud means — a space that users like me and companies use to store data.

## How Was It Before Cloud?

Earlier, development teams used to push their code to their own servers, similar to how we run projects on a terminal or localhost. For production-grade software, companies had their own servers, but not every company can afford that because it is expensive.

## The Concept of Renting Servers

The idea of renting servers existed long ago, but it became a tech thing recently.

For example:  
- Sneha has 100 servers  
- Company 1 needs 10 servers  
- Company 2 needs 20 servers  

So, instead of each company buying their own servers, they rent from Sneha’s servers.

This is basically **cloud computing**.

## What is AWS's Role?

AWS is one of the biggest cloud service providers. When you use AWS services, you are actually using their servers and storage based on your needs.

## Is It Free?

- No, it’s not free.  
- AWS charges based on what you use, how long you use it, and how much you use.  
- This is called **on-demand** and **pay-as-you-go** pricing.
